# ETFE completed-minute VWAP amendment — October 3, 2026

The user authorized replacing the historical bid/ask methodology with option
trade/bar pricing and delegated the choice between high/low midpoint and VWAP.
**Select provider-reported one-minute VWAP (`vw`).** It weights transaction
prices by contracts traded; the high/low midpoint weights two extrema equally.
This is a design choice, not an evaluated claim of better returns or fills.
[Alpaca bar fields](https://alpaca.markets/sdks/python/api_reference/data/models.html),
[historical option bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/optionbars).

This amendment takes precedence over ETFE's earlier inherited quote-price rules.
V7 itself is unchanged. The original scope/setup remain immutable predecessor
evidence; the active [v2 scope](ETFE_SCOPE_VWAP_20261003.json) and new setup bind
the changed methodology under `etfe-option-completed-1min-vwap-v1`.

## Reference price and timing

- Use each contract's **one-minute** option trade-bar VWAP for the option-price
  reference, irrespective of the underlying signal's 1/5/15-minute timeframe.
  This is per-bar VWAP, not cumulative session VWAP or an average of bar VWAPs.
- Require finite positive `vw`, at least one traded contract (`v`) and at least
  one transaction (`n`). No automatic high/low, close, quote or last-trade fallback.
  Missing/invalid references cannot produce fills. Preserve missingness reasons.
- A bar labeled 09:30 summarizes 09:30:00 through just before 09:31:00. It cannot
  supply a reference during that interval. Model earliest availability as
  **bar end + one second**, or a later captured availability timestamp.
  A 09:31:00 signal can first use that bar at 09:31:01.
- Preserve one-second signal-to-entry latency and the 60-second entry deadline.
  Earliest modeled execution is at least `max(signal_close + 1s, available_at)`;
  do not add latency twice or backdate a fill to the reference bar's trades.
- Selected freshness assumption: reference age measured from **bar end** must be
  at most 60 seconds. Do not carry a bar across further missing minutes. This
  replaces the quote-specific 30-second rule; it does not prove the last trade
  occurred within 30 seconds or establish available quoted size.
- Alpaca's final historical aggregates do not establish original publication or
  revision times. The one-second availability lag is an explicit research
  assumption, not measured feed behavior. Admission must address delayed/corrected
  bars and test sensitivity to publication lag and sparse trading.

`nn_meta/etfe/vwap_reference.py` implements this bounded reference policy. Its
caller must bind the contract, 1Min response provenance, session, provider/feed,
and latest eligible bar. The helper returns an observation, not an executable
fill. The independent setup revision is implemented; the full execution engine
and feature mapping are not yet admitted.

## Execution, risk and features

The selected modeling reference for entries, exits, marks and the existing
12.5% premium-stop comparison becomes completed-minute VWAP. Retain 0.10% fees
and 0.05% slippage per side, accounted separately from the reference. Preserve
the declared premium-stop denominator until full ledger validation; do not
redefine it by mixing fees into one side only. A VWAP observation is neither an
order fill nor an assurance that our intended quantity was available.

Evaluate stops at the inherited one-minute observation cadence using eligible
VWAP references. Do not infer a timestamped stop execution from a bar's low/high,
or turn a missing mark into a zero price. Stops, missing held marks, entry expiry,
liquidity/size limits and exit/roll execution need their own validated policies
before replay admission. Failure to obtain a mark does not erase a held liability.

Quote-age and minimum bid/ask-size entry guards are superseded by the bar
reference eligibility rules. Historical quote acquisition is no longer required
for this pricing method. Quote-only feature columns cannot retain their old names
and meanings with fabricated values: create an explicit trade/bar feature mapping
and new schema, including missingness and causal windows. Retain all strategy
families/parameters, thirteen TradingView scopes and other nonquote scope; audit
dependencies before claiming any feature is available. No silent zero filling.

Keep exactly SPY/QQQ/VOO; signals at 1/5/15 minutes; actual 1–9 calendar DTE with
existing family-specific targets; 15-minute pre-close ITM expiry exits; OTM rolls
and funded physical delivery; five shared slots/10% equity fraction; full horizon
purging, ten-session embargo and train-only fitting. Dates remain Alpaca's earliest
options history through October 2, 2026 inclusive. No Massive inputs or V7 models.

## Acceptance boundary

`ETFE.pricing-basis` closes the user choice and implements a tested causal
reference component plus new source-bound setup. It does not close ETFE-0 or
execution admission. Historical boundary, bar/trade coverage, feed provenance,
dated contract/deliverable evidence, feature mapping, causal execution/costs,
folds and measured resources remain gated. No training or data campaign is
authorized merely by changing the reference price.
