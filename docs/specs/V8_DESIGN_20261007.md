# V8 design — October 7, 2026 (proposal, stage-gated)

Status: **proposed**. Only stages 0 and 1 are authorized work. Every later stage
needs its own pre-registration and the user's go-ahead after the previous gate.

## Why a new design

Every generation's apparent edge came from a flaw in its evaluation, not from the
market ([EXPERIMENTS](../EXPERIMENTS.md), October 7 entries):

| Generation | Source of the apparent edge |
|---|---|
| v3 / v3.1 | Leaked future feature `prem_spot` |
| v4 / v5 | Gates redefined after failing; lottery-shaped folds; most recent fold lost |
| v6 | Bid/ask-midpoint fills on illiquid contracts (realistic fills: no profitable configuration or spread bucket) |
| ETFE | Signal-bar fill leak, then intrinsic-value expiry credit |

Direct tests found no edge: pooled ETFE signals on the underlying (±0.6 bp),
tuned-variant walk-forward (in/out-of-sample rank correlation ≈ 0; nothing beat
buy-and-hold), and V5's 415 scored live alerts (−6.4% mean). V6 favored
wide-spread tickers because that is where midpoint pricing manufactured return.

**Principle:** establish an edge on cheap, liquid instruments before any model or
leverage, and make the evaluation unable to pay for fake edge.

## Universe (user preference, 2026-10-07)

Mostly technology names plus ETFs that cover the other sectors.

- **Tech candidate pool (~60):** large, option-liquid technology and
  tech-adjacent names: mega-cap platforms, semiconductors, software,
  security, internet. Examples: AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOGL, META, AMZN, AVGO, ORCL,
  CRM, ADBE, AMD, INTC, QCOM, TXN, MU, AMAT, LRCX, KLAC, TSM, MRVL, ANET, CSCO,
  IBM, NOW, INTU, PANW, CRWD, FTNT, DDOG, SNOW, PLTR, SHOP, UBER, NFLX, TSLA.
- **ETFs:** SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA; sector SPDRs XLK, XLC, XLY, XLP, XLE, XLF, XLV,
  XLI, XLB, XLRE, XLU; tech sub-industry SMH, IGV.
- **Survivorship:** the pool is fixed from information available at the start of
  the development window (market cap and option volume as of that date). A name
  enters only once it is listed with sufficient history. Choosing today's
  winners would bake in hindsight.
- **Admission is data-driven (stage 0):** a candidate is tradable on a day only
  if it passes the liquidity rule on that day. Final membership is an output, not
  a hand-picked list.
- **Benchmarks:** QQQ and XLK for the tech book, SPY for the whole book. A
  tech-heavy universe is a correlated tech-beta bet, so any edge must beat
  these, not cash.

## Stage 0 — tradable universe and cost model

1. **Liquidity rule at decision time:** stock spread and volume floors; for
   options, quoted spread ≤ 3–5% of mid (cutoff chosen from the data and frozen
   before stage 1 results), minimum open interest and volume, and option price
   floor (~$1) so fixed tick spreads don't dominate.
2. **Fill model:** stock fills at the touch plus a slippage allowance; option
   fills at mid ± k × half-spread from measured quotes, with k = 1.0 as the
   primary case (V6 paper fills paid ~0.9 of the half-spread per side) and 0.5
   as a sensitivity. Robinhood fees on top. Midpoint-only results never count.
3. **Data:** stock bars (Alpaca); option quotes from Massive Options Starter
   flat files (2-year history), the source of V6's decision-time spreads.
4. **Deliverable:** a frozen universe/cost contract and a coverage report.

## Stage 1 — edge on the underlying (no options, no meta-model)

- Strategy families on the admitted universe at **daily and multi-day**
  horizons (1h/1d bars), where earlier work looked promising. The intraday SPY/QQQ
  case is closed (ETFE).
- **Test:** the tuned-variant walk-forward used for ETFE on 2026-10-07. Select on
  in-sample Sharpe, score out of sample, expanding window, realistic costs.
- **Pass (all required):** out-of-sample selected variants beat the benchmark
  after costs in most folds; positive in/out-of-sample rank persistence;
  deflated Sharpe clearly above zero; PBO (probability of backtest overfitting)
  below 0.5; survives costs at 1.5×.
- **If nothing passes:** stop directional work and run stage 2 next. No meta-model
  is built on a signal set without edge.

## Stage 2 — volatility (parallel candidate)

The August vol track found realized volatility forecastable (HAR-RV, a standard
daily/weekly/monthly realized-volatility forecast, is the bar to beat) where
direction is not. Candidate: trade implied vs forecast realized volatility on the
liquid ETFs and mega-caps with **defined-risk** structures only (earlier naked
premium selling blew up). It needs its own pre-registration and the same cost
model.

## Stage 3 — instrument mapping

Short horizons (hours to ~2 days) trade the stock/ETF. Options only for
multi-day holds: admitted contracts, delta ~0.5–0.7, expiry matched to the hold.
Stops are based on the underlying, or on the option midpoint, never the fill
price.

## Stage 4 — meta-model (only if stage 1 or 2 passes)

The target is **net-of-cost P&L**. The model must beat the simple stage-1 rule
on realistic fills, and any return head must beat a predict-zero baseline before
it sizes anything.

## Stage 5 — holdout and forward evidence

- Pre-register design, folds and pass rules before results.
- Development window ends 2025-12-31. **2026-01-01 → 2026-10-02 is a single-use
  holdout** for V8. Disclosure: earlier generations evaluated different designs
  on overlapping 2026 data (v6 test April–August; ETFE 2026 test unspent).
- Forward paper trading 6–8 weeks, with a ledger recording entries **and exits**,
  before any capital decision. No gate is redefined after a failure.

## FA workstream (October 7 assessment)

- **Attention signals** (Reddit, StockTwits, news velocity): archive begins about
  July 2026, so they cannot be backtested over 2024–2025. The weekly evaluation
  reports `insufficient-purged-history` (11 labeled days of 50 required). Two
  monthly cohorts exist (August, September); September tracking began October 2.
  **Role:** forward-only shadow overlay logged alongside V8 paper trading,
  judged under FA-3 (three clean forward cohorts). It never gates V8.
- **Fundamentals / short interest / filings** (Massive, 300 tickers): captures
  start September 2026 and their statements are mostly 2025–2026 periods.
  **Role:** candidate stage-1/4 features only after a point-in-time backfill
  keyed to filing/acceptance dates for the V8 universe (~100 names, cheap).
  Not a stage-1 dependency.
