  checks, PIT filtering before joins, atomic output files/completion receipt.
  Schema inference spans all shards; numeric precision is retained.
- Prepared fold arrays shared as read-only memory maps, with training-only
  scaler and winsor bounds matching the reference.
- Independent seed subprocesses, configurable 1–4 workers/threads, a 4 GiB
  available-memory admission guard and hash-verified per-seed receipts.
  This run uses two workers and two threads each.
- Completed final seeds survive interruptions and gate rejection. Changed
  settings or corrupt artifacts fail closed. Ensemble prediction loads one
  member at a time in bounded batches with canonical mean precision.
- Evaluation reads complete mark histories by selected trade identity.
  Shallow split assignment and narrow summaries avoid full-feature copies.

  test, three disjoint test periods, expanding training, existing exit-time
  purging and one-day embargo. Original default fold generation is unchanged.
  Two of its three nominal folds overlap the generator optimization horizon
  on the assembled dataset and would be excluded. These results are conditional: proposals were globally
  optimized through December 19, 2025, not independently refrozen per fold.

Stream assembly can change row ordering among otherwise identical rows versus
the old monolithic sort. This is a new dataset/campaign, not bit-identical
retraining of a historical run. Model-path parity uses identical input order.
