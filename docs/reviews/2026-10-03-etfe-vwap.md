## Verification

`./venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_etfe_vwap.py tests/test_etfe_setup.py -q`
passed **46 tests**. New coverage checks:

- VWAP selection when it differs from high/low midpoint; no OHLC fallback.
- No within-bar or pre-availability access; end + 1s earliest modeled availability.
- Later captured availability overrides the modeled minimum.
- UTC conversion, minute alignment, the 60s bar-end-age boundary, missing bars,
  invalid/nonfinite prices and nonpositive volume/trade count.
- Unchanged dates, universe, DTE, portfolio, entry latency/deadline, costs and risk
  thresholds; implicit fallback, feature fabrication and admission changes rejected.
- Prepared artifact, current source and snapshot mutations rejected by verification.

The actual preparation and both original/successor integrity CLIs pass. Repeating
preparation into the existing root fails before modifying evidence. Predecessor
file hashes and catalog byte identity reconcile.

## Remaining boundary

The historical quote-price requirement is superseded by the accepted amendment.
ETFE-0 remains open for history/coverage, dated contracts, publication assumptions
and explicit quote-feature replacement. Entry/exit/stop execution, liquidity and
cost stress, held-mark gaps, folds and resources must be validated before a build
or training campaign. All readiness flags remain false. No old V7 artifacts,
Massive quotes or model weights were used.
